About The True GOAT

Everyone argues for free. Here it costs something, so it means something.

The True GOAT is the definitive pay-to-rank Hall of Fame. The ranking is the money. Every board shows exactly what fans have put behind each contender, in dollars — no judges, no black-box algorithms, nothing to game.

A free poll measures clicks. Clicks cost nothing, so they prove nothing: a bored afternoon and a script look identical to real belief. This measures conviction, because the only way to move a number here is to put something behind it.

$1 of credit = 1 vote, so the score reads in plain dollars. Add credit once, then back your GOAT with one tap — the board moves the moment you do.

Inside every contender's page sits their Top Fans leaderboard. Back someone enough to take the #1 spot, and you become their Greatest Fan of All Time with your photo and handle crowned on their page.
