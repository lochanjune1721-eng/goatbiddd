About

Everyone argues about this for free. Here it costs something, so it means something.

Fans buy credit, then spend it backing whoever they believe in. The money is the ranking. Inside each person's page is a second leaderboard — their top fans, by total spent.

Dodo charges 4% + $0.40 per transaction. On a $1 bid that's 44% gone. On a $25 top-up it's 5.6%. That's why you top up credit once and then back with one tap — no checkout, no redirect. Tokens, not per-bid cards. Same reason arcades do it.

Before building with Dodo, we confirm this product is approved — stored balances can raise questions.
