  • Equal totals stay in the order reached — earlier one keeps the higher rank.
  • Nothing expires, decays, or is refunded.
  • Anyone can back anyone. Nobody owns a person's board.
  • Every contribution is public and permanent.
  • Fan leaderboard: each person has a board of backers by total spent. Top 3 shown on their card.
