Rules of The True GOAT

Credit & backing — $1 of credit = 1 vote, so every board reads in plain dollars. Add credit once ($5, $10, $20, $50, $100, or custom), then back your favorite contender with one tap. Credit never expires.

  • Rank is the money — Nothing else. No algorithm, no free clicks, no bot brigades. Every dollar on the board is one somebody actually put down.
  • Totals are cumulative — $10 behind someone sitting on $40 puts them at $50 immediately.
  • Equal totals — Stay in the order reached; the earlier contender keeps the higher rank until someone puts more down.
  • Top Fans Leaderboard — Every person's page tracks who has backed them most. The #1 backer is crowned Greatest Fan of All Time.
  • Outbidding — Anyone can outbid the current #1 fan with one tap by spending the exact difference needed from their wallet.
  • Eligible Contenders — Public figures with verifiable Wikipedia entries. No self-promotions or private individuals.
  • Transparency — All totals, rankings, and fan leaderboards are public and permanent.
